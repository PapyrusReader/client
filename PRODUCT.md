## Platform

adaptive

## Product Purpose

Papyrus is an offline-first, cross-platform book library and reader. The reading
statistics page helps readers understand their reading habits. This priority was
confirmed for the statistics redesign.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The Flutter client uses Provider and DataStore. Preserve guest, user and server
profile isolation and durable reading activity. Statistics must reflect existing
provider calculations, with explicit labels for different date scopes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve light, dark and e-ink themes, reduced motion, keyboard access, text scaling
and responsive layouts. Follow existing design tokens and shared controls.

## Open Decisions

No broader audience or brand changes were requested in this page redesign.
